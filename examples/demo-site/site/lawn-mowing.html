Lawn mowing with a fixed quote

Brightline Lawn Care mows small and mid-size residential lawns on a fixed weekly or fortnightly schedule. We send one quote before the first visit, based on the measured size of the lawn, and the price stays the same all season unless the lawn changes. Our crews mow, trim the edges and blow the paths clean on every visit.

How the price is set

We measure the lawn from a satellite map before we quote, so the price follows the real mowing area. Our guide to mowing height explains why we cut at the same setting all season, and why that rarely changes the price.

What every visit includes

Each crew follows the same short list, so the lawn looks the same whoever comes. We start with the edges, then mow in a new direction each week to keep the grass standing straight.

  • Mowing at the height set for your grass type, see the right height for each season.
  • Edging along paths and driveways, and a clear price for any extra bed trimming.
  • Blowing clippings off paths so nothing is tracked indoors.

How a season works

  1. Request a quote. Tell us your address through the contact form and we measure the lawn.
  2. Approve the price. We send one number, and you can read who we are before you agree.
  3. Relax. A text tells you the day before every visit, and the crew leaves a note when it is done.

Most customers stay with us for several seasons because the whole service is built around the schedule, not the sales call. A crew that mows the same street every week learns where the soft ground, the sprinkler heads and the dog runs are, and that knowledge is the real reason a fixed route beats a different crew each time. When something changes, such as a new flower bed or a lawn that has been reseeded, you tell us once and every later visit follows the note.

Questions about mowing

5 questions ·

  1. How much does a lawn mowing visit cost?

    The price depends on the size of the lawn, and we quote it before the first visit. A small front yard is the cheapest visit; a corner lot with slopes costs more, as our price table shows.

  2. How often should the lawn be mowed?

    Weekly in spring and early summer, then every other week when growth slows. We set the rhythm with you at the first visit, using our mowing height by season as the starting point.

  3. Do you bag the clippings?

    No, we leave the clippings on the lawn by default because they feed the grass. We bag them on request when the grass is long or wet.

  4. What happens when it rains on a mowing day?

    We move the visit to the next dry day and tell you by text before we come. You never pay for a visit that did not happen.

  5. Can I pause the service while I am away?

    Yes, send us the dates through the contact page and we skip those visits. Nothing else changes when you come back.

What is the best height to mow a lawn?

Updated 2026-11-10 · by Brightline Lawn Care

Most lawns do best when the grass is cut to about three inches. At that height the leaves are long enough to feed the roots and shade the soil, so weeds find less light to grow in. Cut the grass shorter in a hot dry spell and the lawn browns quickly.

Why the height matters

Grass uses its leaves to make food. A mower that removes more than a third of the blade in one pass slows the roots for a week or more, which is why a weekly mow beats a long gap followed by a hard cut.

Height by season

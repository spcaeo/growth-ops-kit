# Brightline Lawn Care: brand facts (made-up example)

Brightline Lawn Care is a made-up business, used by the kit's tests and examples. Nothing here is real.

## Who we are
- A small, crew-led lawn service for small and mid-size residential lawns.
- Short name: Brightline. Site: https://brightline.example.

## The one call to action
- The one button label on every page is "Get a quote". It goes to /contact.
- The reason line beside a button says what happens next: we reply within one working day with a fixed price.

## Crew times (from our own time sheets)
- A small lawn (under 2,000 sq ft) takes 20 minutes, a medium one takes 35 minutes, a large one takes 60 minutes.

## Words we do not use
- "Free estimate" and "no obligation" (we quote, and the quote is the offer).
- Hype words: "revolutionary", "seamless", "best in class".

## What we never claim
- A "guaranteed lowest price".
- Customer names, counts or reviews (none are approved).

## Voice
- Plain words, short sentences, US spelling.

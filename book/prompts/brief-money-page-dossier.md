# Brief: research dossier for one money page (read-only)

You are the senior researcher for ONE money page: __PAGE__ (read its source and your local build, never the live
site). A money page is a bottom-of-funnel page: its reader is close to the one conversion ("__CTA__"). This is research
only: you write ONE file and change nothing else.

Output: `__PROJECT__/research/__SLUG__-dossier-__DATE__.md`

## Why
Each money page has to be unique. It has to understand the core problem of its reader: who they are, why they are
searching, what they fear, and what content is needed to cover the depth without duplicating the guides and blog
posts. Study the actual content of the top 10 to 20 competitors, slowly, one at a time. Do it because it improves
conversion and engagement, not because it is on a list. Go slowly and deeply. Quality over speed. The questions to
answer are Part 1 of the book's `playbooks/money-page.md` (questions 1 to 36).

## What to research (in this order)
1. **Our own data.** This page's impressions, position, clicks and the searches behind them from Search Console and
   analytics through your connector, or the page tracker. Otherwise write "no search data yet" and say what the first
   read should pull. Never guess traffic; never print the keys.
2. **The buyer.** 3 to 5 buyer types (role, size, the trade, the tool or mess they use now, who pays vs who uses it),
   each with: the core problem, the trigger ("why now"), what they fear, what they need to believe to convert, and
   their words.
3. **The top 10 to 20 Google results** for the page's core searches (reuse `research/serp/`; fetch new ones only with
   the tool the lead names; at most 12 queries). READ EACH competitor page in full, one at a time (a page behind a bot
   check is never read by ANY route: note it and move on). For each: who they are, the promise on the first screen,
   every fold in order, proof, the ask, interaction, what they do well, what they leave unanswered.
4. **Our content map (no duplication).** List what our posts and pages already cover that this buyer needs. For each:
   what the money page should only link to (one line plus the link) and what only the money page should say.
5. **What we can truthfully claim.** From `SETUP.md`, `BRAND.md`, and the live site's own approved copy. NOT allowed:
   case studies or customer names, prices not on record, anything `BRAND.md` forbids, invented reviewers, rating
   schema.

## What to propose (for the owner to choose; build nothing)
- **The core hook:** 2 to 3 options, each one sentence a buyer would feel, and why it beats the competitors' first
  screens.
- **The journey, fold by fold:** 8 to 12 folds. Per fold: the buyer's question at that moment, the answer, the proof,
  the small step that makes them scroll or act, and an interaction idea. Every fold a different experience; nothing
  shared by default with other pages.
- **A signature interaction** unique to this page, how it works, what it asks, what it shows, how it leads to the
  conversion, and the URL state it keeps.
- **The ask ladder:** for each buyer type, the right next step within what the business offers (a new free asset goes
  under "Needs the owner").
- **Searches to own** (the page's title, H1, first-screen promise) and **searches to leave** to other pages.
- **What to measure** after launch (impressions, position, clicks for the target searches; readers; conversions).

## Also cover
(1) A buyer maturity ladder. (2) The pain behind the search, with real quotes and links where readable. (3) The
internal sale: what the person who found the business needs to win over the person who decides. (4) Geography: where
the readers are (rules differ by region; do not assume, state what the data shows).

## Rules
Paste Block 2 (READ-ONLY RULES) from the README.

## Report (simple English)
The 3 biggest findings, the recommended hook, the signature interaction, and anything that needs the owner (for
example a claim we would need approved). Then end yourself.
